MapMaker Web Map Auditor

Self-check for distributor and partner web maps against the General Cartographic Standards

Public items only. Paste an item ID (a1b2…), an arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=… URL, a viewer URL with webmap=, or a group ID to audit every public web map in the group. Deep-link for ArcGIS Hub: ?group=<id>&run=1, ?id=<id>&run=1, or ?ids=a,b,c&run=1.

Rendering the map for the pixel checks. Keep this browser tab in front until it finishes.
This tool automates the mechanical parts of review. Guideline checks marked warn are heuristics — a map may pass review with warnings, and the standards note that maps should still be reviewed by an NGS or Esri cartographer before publication. Nothing here is a substitute for that. The overall score and letter grade are a convenience roll-up of the checks (fail-severity items weighted more than guideline warnings; advisory info items are not scored) and do not override the verdict. Colour checks use CIEDE2000 (ISO/CIE 11664-6) colour difference and the Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009) colour-vision-deficiency simulation; thresholds are screening heuristics. Colour scheme guidance via ColorBrewer (Brewer & Harrower, Penn State). The Rendered map checks draw the web map at its saved extent with the ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript, take a screenshot with and without the data layers, and compare the two: data coverage is the share of the viewport where the data layers change the pixels, and basemap contrast is the median CIEDE2000 difference between each drawn data pixel and the basemap beneath it. These are screening heuristics: deliberately transparent layers score lower, and basemap labels can shift slightly when data labels are removed.
