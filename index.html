MapMaker Web Map Auditor

Self-check for distributor and partner web maps against the General Cartographic Standards

Public items only. Paste an item ID (a1b2…), an arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=… URL, a viewer URL with webmap=, or a group ID to audit every public web map in the group. Deep-link for ArcGIS Hub: ?group=<id>&run=1, ?id=<id>&run=1, or ?ids=a,b,c&run=1.

This tool automates the mechanical parts of review. Guideline checks marked warn are heuristics — a map may pass review with warnings, and the standards note that maps should still be reviewed by an NGS or Esri cartographer before publication. Nothing here is a substitute for that. The overall score and letter grade are a convenience roll-up of the checks (fail-severity items weighted more than guideline warnings; advisory info items are not scored) and do not override the verdict. Colour checks use CIEDE2000 (ISO/CIE 11664-6) colour difference and the Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009) colour-vision-deficiency simulation; thresholds are screening heuristics. Colour scheme guidance via ColorBrewer (Brewer & Harrower, Penn State).
